Picking the right packaging for your online store isn't just about getting your products from point A to point B. It's about protecting what's inside, managing your shipping costs, and increasingly, making choices that align with your brand's environmental values. When it comes to lightweight shipping options, poly mailers and bubble mailers are two popular choices that online retailers often debate between.
