In the competitive landscape of consumer packaged goods (CPG), efficient and flexible logistics are paramount. Bulu, headquartered in Lincoln, Nebraska, has been at the forefront of fulfillment innovation for over 12 years. Transitioning from a subscription box pioneer to a leading logistics provider, Bulu now offers tech-enabled 5PL services at 3PL rates, empowering CPG brands with comprehensive storage, packing, and shipping solutions across multiple sales channels.
