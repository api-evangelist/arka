At the heart of Bryan Davidson’s work with Whisper is a powerful idea: that connection is an essential human need. Based in the Atlanta area, Whisper is a nonprofit devoted to combating the growing loneliness epidemic among students. Through innovative conversation tools and leadership programs, Whisper empowers students across the country to lead movements of connection in their schools—and they’re just getting started.
