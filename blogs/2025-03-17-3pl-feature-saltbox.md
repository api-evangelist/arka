Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) have long struggled to find logistics solutions that cater to their unique needs. Traditional third-party logistics (3PL) providers often impose high costs, rigid contracts, and restrictive volume requirements, making it difficult for growing brands to scale efficiently. Saltbox is redefining logistics by offering a 3PL alternative, providing co-warehousing and co-working spaces designed specifically for SMBs.
