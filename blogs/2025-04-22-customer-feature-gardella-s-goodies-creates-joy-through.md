Gardella’s Goodies isn’t just a dessert brand—it’s a gifting experience built around connection, surprise, and joy. Based in Ithaca, New York, the company specializes in handmade cookies, oversized chocolate truffles, and thoughtfully themed treat boxes for life’s many moments. Gardella's Goodies makes it easy to send sweetness in a box, whether it’s a birthday, a new job, or a pop-culture celebration.
