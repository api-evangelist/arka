When Biobox Labs set out to make science more accessible, they had one mission: create experiences that spark curiosity and wonder in kids, right at home. Founded by Lisa, a longtime science educator and professor, Biobox Labs offers a subscription service that delivers life science experiments straight to families' doorsteps each month. “We just found that kids weren't getting a chance to do a lot of hands-on experiments when they were younger,” Lisa explains.
