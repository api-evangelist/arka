At Beau Ties of Vermont , the story begins with a simple belief: great style deserves great presentation. For over 30 years, this Vermont-based artisan has been handcrafting bow ties, neckties, and pocket squares that showcase individuality and quality. But recently, they discovered that what goes inside the box is only half the story, what goes around it matters just as much.
